# Callout card

Print this page and keep it beside you. Rules: [spec §6.1](spec/06-spatial-language-and-radar.md), [§7.2–7.4](spec/07-cooperation-pairs.md).

**Say it like this:** `<who>, <verb> <object> [<qualifier>]`. The listener answers **"copy"**.

> "Helm, face H2." "Copy."

"Verbal" means same-room speech. The game has no voice chat; remote players need their own.

## Names

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| `H2` `U3` `F1` `N4` | Contact callsign: hostile, unknown, friendly, neutral. The number never changes. |
| Alpha to Hotel | Your ship's 8 marks. Anyone can place one. |
| `Kestrel Yard` | A site. Everyone shares its name. |
| `12` bow, `3` starboard, `6` stern, `9` port | Clock, in the ship frame. Same on every station. |
| `level`, `high`, `low` | Height. Say `4 high`. `overhead` and `under` need no clock. |
| `close`, `mid`, `far` | Under 1 km, 1 to 10 km, over 10 km. |

Name things, don't point. Away from your ship, say it from a mark: `Bravo, 12 high, 3 km`. Add `on my ping` when you pinged it.

## Pairs

Any shortcut you don't have is done by voice. A request chip is a shortcut for a call. Press Share (Alt+S) to show your data to your partner for 30 s.

## Habits

- Short and loud. One order per call.
- Always answer "copy". No answer means say it again.
- Ping an item someone already pinged: that is a silent "copy".
- Can't say it? Say the callsign and the clock.
